    How the math works

    1. Trips are counted first, at the resort you picked. Each day is covered if your pass includes that resort, the day limit isn't used up, and it isn't a blacked-out holiday. Otherwise you pay that resort's day ticket.
    2. Local days go to the nearest resort your pass covers with no day limit, then to resorts with limited days. If none is left, you pay for the cheapest day ticket within your drive.
    3. Holiday days are counted against local days first. Epic Local blacks out holidays at Vail, Beaver Creek, Park City, Heavenly, Northstar, Kirkwood, Stowe and Whistler. Ikon Base blacks out everywhere except Arapahoe Basin.
    4. Combos (like Epic Local + Ikon Base) use whichever pass covers each day. Their price is both passes added together.
    5. Savings = what all your days would cost in day tickets, minus the season cost of that option.

    Pass prices are the listed 2026–27 adult rates as of October 2026, and they rise until sales close. Day-ticket prices are rough online estimates; walk-up prices are often higher, so real savings are usually bigger. Resort stats are approximate. Drive times are estimated from distance.

    Indy Pass is sold out for 2026–27, so it isn't compared. Check each pass's official site before you buy.
